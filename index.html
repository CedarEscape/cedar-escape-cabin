Good to Know

Questions.

What's the bedroom count?
Five bedrooms across two floors, including a walkout basement, with 9 beds total between the bedrooms and bunk room. There's also a downstairs sleeper sofa on top of that, handy if you've got a young child you'd rather not put on a top bunk.
What's the max occupancy?
Sleeps up to 14 guests comfortably.
Is it kid-friendly?
Yes. We offer several family-friendly amenities including a high chair, Pack 'n Play, toddler diningware, child safety gates, and bunk beds. Please note that the property is not fully childproofed, and adult supervision is required at all times.
What time is check-in and check-out?
Check-in is at 4:00 PM, check-out is at 11:00 AM.
Where do we park?
The driveway comfortably fits 3–4 cars. Show up with more, and free overflow parking's got you covered.
What's your cancellation policy?
Cancellation terms are shown before you confirm your reservation. For added peace of mind, optional travel insurance is also available during checkout.
Are pets allowed?
No, we're not able to accommodate pets at this time.
Is smoking allowed?
No, Cedar Escape is a smoke-free property.
