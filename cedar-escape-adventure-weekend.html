Adventure Weekend FAQ

Quick answers for planning your trip.

How far is Shenandoah National Park from the house?
The closest entrance (Swift Run Gap) is roughly a 35-minute drive — close enough for a full day hike without losing the morning to driving.
Do we need to leave the resort for a real hike?
Not necessarily — there are two real trails on-property (Kaylor's Knob and the Massanutten Ridge Trail), plus Shenandoah National Park a short drive away if you want more.
Is mountain biking available on-site?
Yes — Massanutten has its own Mountain Bike Park with terrain for a range of skill levels, plus e-bike pedal-assist if your group wants it.
