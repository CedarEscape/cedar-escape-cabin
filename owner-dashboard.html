Action Plan

Concrete levers to increase visibility

These are the factors Airbnb and Booking.com's own ranking algorithms are documented to reward. Not guesses — this is what actually moves the "how often are we shown" number.

  • 1 Respond to every inquiry fast — under an hour if possible. Both platforms factor response time directly into ranking, and the Oct 2–4 triple-inquiry above suggests speed may already be costing you contested dates.
  • 2 Keep the calendar wide open and accurate. Blocked/stale dates reduce how often you're surfaced in search.
  • 3 Turn on Instant Book wherever you're comfortable. Both algorithms favor frictionless booking.
  • 4 Actively request a review after every single stay. You're at 1 review — this is the highest-leverage action available right now.
  • 5 Respond publicly to every review (even the great one you have). Engagement signals matter to ranking and to future guests reading it.
  • 6 Re-check pricing against comparable Massanutten listings for weeks with low inquiry volume — underpricing and overpricing both suppress visibility differently (overpriced = filtered out of searches with a budget; underpriced = you sell out and stop appearing at all).
  • 7 Keep listing content (photos, description, amenities) fully filled out on every platform — incomplete listings are down-ranked on both Airbnb and Booking.com.
