A Sample Family Weekend Itinerary

Three Days. A Happier You.

Here's one way your family getaway at Cedar Escape can come together — as inspiration, never a required schedule.

1
Friday · Arrive & Unwind

The Vacation Starts Here.

Check in, get comfortable, and let the kids explore the game room. Order dinner or make an easy meal, then settle in for popcorn and a family movie night.

2
Saturday · Adventure Day

Make a Splash. Make Memories.

Spend the day at Massanutten's WaterPark or choose a seasonal adventure like hiking, skiing, or snow tubing. Head back to Cedar Escape for a delicious family dinner from one of our local food partners, followed by games and movie night.

3
Sunday · One More Memory

Slow Mornings Are Part of It.

Enjoy breakfast together, take a few family photos, play a final game, or fit in one last local adventure before heading home with full hearts and stories everyone will be talking about.

Family Getaway FAQ

Quick answers for planning your trip.

Is Cedar Escape good for multi-generational trips?
Yes — five bedrooms and 9 beds mean grandparents, parents, and kids can each have their own space instead of sharing a hotel room.
What's actually here for young kids?
A high chair, toddler kitchenware, a pack n' play, a toddler toilet seat, gates, and bunk beds.
Is there room for the kids to have their own space?
Yes — the bunk room and downstairs sleeper sofa give kids somewhere separate from the adult bedrooms, plus a movie room and game room for their own hangout space.
Are activities and meals included in my reservation?
No. WaterPark, skiing and snow tubing, workshops and meals from local businesses are arranged and paid for separately with each provider. Hours, ages and availability vary, so check each website before you go.
