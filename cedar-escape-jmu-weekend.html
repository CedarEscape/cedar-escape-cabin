JMU Weekend FAQ

Quick answers for planning your trip.

How far is Cedar Escape from James Madison University?
About 25 minutes by car. Home football weekends can add time in traffic, so leave a little extra.
Is Cedar Escape suitable for graduation weekends?
Yes. With 5 bedrooms, 9 beds and room for up to 14 guests, extended family can stay together under one roof. Graduation weekends book early, so check availability as soon as your dates are set.
Can multiple families stay together?
Yes. The house has 5 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms and several shared living spaces, and sleeps up to 14 guests, which suits two or three families traveling together.
Are catering options available?
Yes. Pop's Southern Cuisine, one of our local partners, offers catering for groups and events, and Crossroads Café & Catering is another local option. Contact the caterer directly to plan your menu; catering is priced separately and subject to their availability. You can also browse more options in our local catering guide.
What is there to do near Cedar Escape beyond JMU?
Massanutten Resort, downtown Harrisonburg, Shenandoah National Park, and local wineries are all a short drive away. See our Explore Massanutten guide and Wine Weekend page for ideas.
How do I check availability for football or graduation weekends?
Use the booking calendar on our availability page to enter your dates and see live pricing. Football and graduation weekends fill early, so book as soon as you know your dates.
Is there space for parents to work remotely during the trip?
Yes — two dedicated work nooks with high-speed Wi-Fi, so a parent can take a call without disrupting the rest of the group.
