5 Things to Do in Massanutten This Winter

Quick picks for snowy adventures, cozy experiences, local flavors, and beautiful Shenandoah Valley views.

Presented by Cedar Escape

Published 2026-10-30

Home / Things to Do / The Cedar Escape Five

Winter in the Shenandoah Valley isn't only for skiers. Here are five easy ways to enjoy the season, from snow tubing and an indoor splash to pottery, a plate of mofongo and a quiet look at the mountains, with a few planning notes so nothing catches you off guard.

01Illustrative photo

Snow Much Fun at Massanutten Resort

Massanutten is the Valley's winter headquarters, with skiing, snowboarding and a ski and snowboard school. If you'd rather stay off the slopes, snow tubing is the crowd-pleaser: 90-minute sessions that include the tube and the lift ride.

  • Tubing tickets are sold online in advance and regularly sell out. More are released on Wednesday evenings as snow conditions allow.
  • The resort currently lists tubing from mid-December to early March, weather permitting. Dates and hours can change, so check conditions before you go.

02Illustrative photo of an indoor water park

Make a Splash, Even in Winter

When it's too cold to be outside, Massanutten's indoor WaterPark keeps everyone moving. The resort says it operates most days year-round, which makes it an easy plan for mixed-age groups: there's a Frog Pond for guests under 42 inches, and taller swimmers have the slides.

  • Some weekdays may be closed in off-peak seasons, so check the hours calendar first.
  • Online tickets must be bought at least a day ahead and cost less than same-day tickets. The resort recommends buying ahead on weekends and holidays.
WaterPark hours & ratesWaterPark overview
03

Get Creative at Friendly City Clay

Friendly City Clay & Art Center in Harrisonburg welcomes complete beginners, with wheel, handbuilding and family-friendly workshops. Check the current schedule for dates that fit your stay, since a few sessions are waitlist-only.

  • Pieces are fired and glazed by the studio, so they are typically ready for pickup several weeks later. Timing varies by class, so ask before you sign up if you're leaving town soon.
  • Booked guests: see the Cedar Escape digital guidebook for redemption details.
Current classesStudio website
04

Find Your New Favorite Local Flavor

Brisas del Caribe 809 in Elkton serves Dominican-inspired favorites, including empanadas and mofongo. It's a flavorful stop roughly 12 to 13 minutes from the resort.

  • Menus change, so check the restaurant's page before you go. It's at 11702 Spotswood Trail in Elkton.
  • Sweet tooth? Ask whether they have tres leches cake available.
Brisas on FacebookGet directions
05

Discover a Different Side of the Mountains

Winter strips Shenandoah National Park down to quiet trails, long views and frosted woods. When there's snow or ice, Skyline Drive may close, but the park itself stays open for hiking, snowshoeing and cross-country skiing.

  • Skyline Drive is normally open 24 hours, but severe weather can close it at any time. Watch for ice on shaded curves; the speed limit is 35 mph and conditions often call for slower.
  • Visitor services and cell coverage are very limited, so pack layers, food and water. Text SHENALERTS to 888777 for closure alerts.
