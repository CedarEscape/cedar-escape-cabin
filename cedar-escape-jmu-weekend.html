JMU Weekend FAQ

Quick answers for planning your trip.

How far is Cedar Escape from JMU?
Roughly a 25-30 minute drive depending on traffic, which is common during home football weekends.
Is this a good option for football weekends?
Yes — with 9 beds and space for up to 14, it's an easy way to host extended family or a group of friends for a home game without splitting across hotels. Just book early, since fall weekends go fast.
Is there space for parents to work remotely during the trip?
Yes — two dedicated work nooks with high-speed Wi-Fi, so a parent can take a call without disrupting the rest of the group.
