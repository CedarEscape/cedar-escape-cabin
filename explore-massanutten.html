Inside Massanutten Resort

Everything the resort offers.

Cedar Escape sits inside the Massanutten Resort community — a real booking advantage, not a claim that these are included with your stay. Resort activities are separate, paid passes.

Skiing

Slopes right on the mountain, seasonal.

WaterPark

Indoor/outdoor slides, Diamond Jim's Arcade, Splash's Bar & Grill.

Golf

Mountain Greens and Woodstone Meadows, both 18 holes.

Dining

Base Camp, Campfire Grill, Splash's, Elevations Lounge, Kline's Dairy Bar.

Adventure Park

Zip-lining, tubing, chairlift rides, mountain biking, go-karts, mini golf.

Cedar's Local Picks

Where we'd actually send you.

Not a directory — just the spots worth the drive.

Restaurants

Best For
A night off from cooking for the whole group.
Cedar Tip
Wood Grill Buffet in Harrisonburg is the easiest way to feed a big, hungry group — everyone finds something.

Wineries + Breweries

Best For
Wine plus lunch for a group, or a girls' weekend.
Cedar Tip
CrossKeys Vineyards' bistro simplifies planning. Brix & Columns, Marceline, and The Purple Wolf are all nearby too.

Family Fun

Best For
Kids who've had enough of the game room.
Cedar Tip
Go-karts, mini golf, Spin Zone bumper cars, and Real Escapes' VR experiences — all inside the resort.

Hikes + Views

Best For
An easy morning out before everyone's back at the house.
Cedar Tip
The Massanutten Storybook Trail is family-friendly with real payoff. Shenandoah National Park is about 35 minutes for a bigger day.

JMU + Harrisonburg

Best For
Game days, campus visits, and a coffee run into town.
Cedar Tip
7 Brew Coffee on the way in, then downtown Harrisonburg for local theater and galleries.
