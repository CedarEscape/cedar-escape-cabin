Wine Weekend FAQ

Quick answers for planning your trip.

How far are the wineries from the house?
Most are within a 10–25 minute drive, and several sit close enough together to visit more than one in the same day.
What makes Cedar Escape perfect for a girls' weekend?
It gives old friends the space to make new memories—long conversations, loud laughter, slow mornings, and the kind of late nights nobody wants to end.
What if we don't want to plan every minute?
You don't have to. Some of the best moments happen around the kitchen island or piled into the movie room catching up. Bring your favorite people and let the weekend unfold.
