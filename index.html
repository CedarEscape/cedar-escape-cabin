Staying at Cedar Escape Massanutten was an absolute highlight of our trip to Massanutten Resort! The location could not be better for accessing all the resort amenities, and the home itself was impeccably clean, spacious, and wonderfully family-friendly (having a high chair provided was a huge plus for families with little ones). With 5 large bedrooms—including two set up with multiple beds—our group comfortably slept nine people with plenty of room to spare. The main floor living area is beautifully open, making it perfect whether you're entertaining the whole crew or just unwinding after a full day outdoors. On top of the central heat and AC, the owners thoughtfully installed individual mini-split units in the lower level, game room, and theater room, ensuring every single space stayed at the perfect temperature. The amenities here are top-tier: • Endless Entertainment: A dedicated theater room, a full game room with a pool table, card table, classic Ms. Pac-Man arcade machine, exercise space, and even an Oculus VR setup! • Outdoor Living: A premium Traeger grill fully stocked with all the necessary cooking utensils, paired with tons of outdoor seating for sunny group meals. Even though we were only there for a weekend, we created unforgettable memories together. Cedar Escape Massanutten truly exceeded our expectations, and we will definitely book another stay!
